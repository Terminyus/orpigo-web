# Copyright checklist

Rule used (Turkey, FSEK art. 27): protection lasts for the author's life + 70 years, counted from the
start of the year after death. Authors who died in **1955 or earlier** are public domain in 2026.
Translations are separate works, so foreign classics appear **only in English (original)**; the Turkish
version of the site uses Turkish authors only. No song lyrics, no living or recent authors.
